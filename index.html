What Atọ́nà Does

One agent. Every channel.
One memory that compounds.

Atọ́nà handles communication, scheduling, research, and tasks across every channel — with a cross-channel memory that gets smarter over time.

🧠

Morning Brief

Every day at 7am: inbox summary, calendar, weather, commute ETA, and what Atọ́nà handled overnight. Delivered to your phone.

✉️

Email on Autopilot

Monitors your inbox. Auto-replies when confident, holds drafts when not. Sensitive emails never touch AI. Labels and archives the rest.

💬

Texts on Your Behalf

Sends SMS and WhatsApp from your dedicated assistant number. Confirms meetings, relays messages, follows up with your contacts.

📞

Answers Your Phone

Known contacts get the full assistant. Unknown callers get voicemail. You get a transcript, summary, and the conversation remembered.

🎙️

Joins Your Meetings

Joins Zoom, Meet, or Teams. Listens, takes notes, responds when addressed by name. Afterwards: summary, action items, key decisions — sent automatically.

📅

Calendar Intelligence

Creates, updates, and cancels events with attendees and video links. Every channel sees your schedule and can propose free slots automatically.

✅

Delegated Tasks

Create tasks by chat or voice. Atọ́nà executes them autonomously — sending emails, scheduling meetings, doing research — then reports back.

🔍

Deep Research

Multi-angle web research with AI synthesis. Delivered as a structured report to your email, Google Docs, or Notion. Quick and deep modes.

🍽️

Reservations

Search restaurants by cuisine or vibe. Atọ́nà calls the restaurant, books a table, and adds it to your calendar. Fully autonomous.

🧭

Life Navigator

Focus block suggestions for calendar gaps. Follow-up tracking with auto-resolution. Surfaces only what needs your attention. Everything else is handled.

✈️

Flight Intelligence

Detects bookings from email. Tracks gate, terminal, seat, delays. Texts your driver the new arrival time before you even check.

🔒

Privacy First

200+ sensitive patterns classified before any AI processes them. Bank, medical, legal content never leaves your infrastructure. Your data stays yours.
