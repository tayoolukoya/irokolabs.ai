# irokolabs.ai

The website for [Iroko Labs](https://irokolabs.ai).

## Mission

The AI revolution is here — but it wasn't built for everyone.

Most AI products today are designed for people who already understand technology: prompt engineers, developers, power users. The barrier to entry — cost, complexity, and assumed technical literacy — leaves the majority of people behind.

Iroko Labs exists to change that.

We build thoughtfully designed AI products that reduce the barrier to entry, bridge the gap between what AI can do and who can actually use it, and carry everyone along in this revolution — not just the privileged few.

Our belief is simple: the technologies that truly change the world don't ask people to adapt to them. They adapt to people.

## Our First Product

**[Atọ́nà](https://atona.ai)** — a personal AI agent that lives on your phone, remembers everything you've said and emailed, and handles your tasks so you don't have to. No prompting. No workflow setup. Just delegation.

### The Name

**Atọ́nà** carries meaning across two cultures:

- **Yoruba** — *"The one who shows the way."* An AI agent that guides you through your day — not another tool you have to learn.
- **Japanese (吾汝)** — *"Me and you."* A deep personal connection — an assistant that knows you, acts for you, and grows with you.

Named by Iroko Labs, rooted in the cultures that inspire us.
