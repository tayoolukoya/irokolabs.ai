Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 9, 2026

Iroko Labs ("we," "us," or "our") operates Atọ́nà (FKA Nexa), a personal AI agent, and the website irokolabs.ai (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our Service.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information: When you sign up, we collect your name, email address, and phone number to create and manage your account.

Connected Account Data: When you connect services like Gmail or Google Calendar, Atọ́nà accesses your email, calendar events, and contacts to provide its core functionality (email triage, scheduling, briefings). We access only the data necessary to deliver the Service.

Communication Data: Messages you send and receive through Atọ́nà via iMessage, SMS, voice calls, and email are processed to provide the Service. This includes message content, timestamps, and contact information.

Usage Data: We collect information about how you interact with the Service, including feature usage, session duration, and error logs to improve performance.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the Service
  • Process your emails, messages, and calendar events as instructed
  • Generate daily briefings and handle tasks on your behalf
  • Send you service-related communications via SMS, email, or push notifications
  • Respond to your inquiries and provide customer support
  • Detect, prevent, and address technical issues

3. Sensitive Data Protection

Atọ́nà is built with a privacy-first architecture. Before any AI model processes your data, our sensitivity classifier scans for 200+ patterns associated with sensitive content, including:

  • Financial data (bank statements, tax documents, investment accounts)
  • Medical and health information
  • Legal documents and communications
  • Government correspondence
  • Social Security numbers and other personal identifiers

Emails and messages matching these patterns are never sent to any third-party AI provider. They are flagged and excluded from AI processing entirely.

4. Third-Party AI Providers

To provide the Service, non-sensitive data may be processed by third-party AI providers (such as Anthropic and other large language model providers) for tasks including email summarization, auto-reply generation, and research. We select providers with strong data handling practices and do not permit them to use your data for training their models.

5. SMS and Messaging

By using the Service, you consent to receive SMS messages from Atọ́nà (operated by Iroko Labs) at the phone number you provide. These messages may include:

  • Daily briefings and recap summaries
  • Task notifications and follow-up reminders
  • Responses to your requests via text
  • Service alerts and account notifications

Message frequency varies based on your usage and preferences. Message and data rates may apply.

To opt out: Reply STOP to any SMS message to unsubscribe from all text communications. Reply HELP for support. You may also adjust notification preferences within the app or by contacting us at tayo@irokolabs.ai.

6. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on secure servers with encryption at rest and in transit. We implement industry-standard security measures including access controls, audit logging, and regular security reviews. Account credentials for connected services (Gmail, Google Calendar) are stored using encryption and are never accessible in plaintext.

7. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service. If you delete your account, we will delete your personal data within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it by law.

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share data only in the following circumstances:

  • Service Providers: With third-party providers who help us operate the Service (cloud hosting, AI processing, payment processing), subject to confidentiality obligations
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, regulation, or legal process
  • Safety: To protect the rights, safety, or property of Iroko Labs, our users, or the public

9. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Delete your account and associated data
  • Export your data in a portable format
  • Opt out of SMS communications at any time
  • Revoke access to connected services (Gmail, Calendar) at any time through your account settings or your Google account

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at tayo@irokolabs.ai.

10. Children's Privacy

The Service is not intended for individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that we have collected data from a child under 13, we will delete it promptly.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the updated policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date. Your continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
